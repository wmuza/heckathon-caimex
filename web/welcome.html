Mukoti

Mukoti is Shona for nurse.

The first person every patient talks to at the clinic, in Shona or English. It listens, asks up to six short questions aloud, and gives the nurse a note in English and a queue with the most urgent patient at the top.

The name

Named for the person it helps

Mukoti is Shona for nurse. The product is the nurse's helper, and when it speaks to a patient it calls itself mubatsiri, the helper.

It does not diagnose and does not advise treatment. It is a quick check at reception, and the nurse decides. If a patient says a danger sign, it stops asking and calls the nurse.

The first thing a patient hears

Mhoroi, mauya kukiriniki. Ndiri mubatsiri wemukoti, handisi chiremba.

"Hello, welcome to the clinic. I am the nurse's helper, not a doctor."

How it works

From the bench to the nurse

Every question comes with a picture, so a patient who cannot read is not left out. The nurse's queue uses the same pictures.

  1. The patient speaks

    At the door, on a phone or tablet, in Shona or English.

  2. Up to six short questions

    Asked aloud with a picture, and nothing asked twice that was already answered.

  3. A danger sign stops the talk

    Fixed rules, with no AI, check every sentence. An emergency calls the nurse at once.

  4. The nurse gets the note

    A short note in English and a queue ordered by urgency. Only the nurse can lower a level.
