Motion between and within states

Calm, purposeful transitions guide attention without rushing or startling the patient.

1. How a pictogram arrives

When a new question starts, the illustration enters in 440 ms (fade + 12 px lift + gentle settle) while the continuous 3-second loop is already running.

Muri kupisa muviri here?

2. How the microphone invites speech

A slow 2.8 s blue breathing halo invites a tap. Recording shifts to a clear red stop button with a steady pulse; thinking settles all motion.

Dzvanyai maikorofoni mutaure

3. Progress without relying on numbers

Completed steps fill solid blue, the current step widens nearly 2× with a pulsing dot, and upcoming steps wait quietly in neutral grey.
