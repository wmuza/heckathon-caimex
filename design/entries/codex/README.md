# Mukoti patient experience

One picture shows what the helper means, and a microphone in the same
place hands the turn to the patient. The ticket keeps the patient's
number in place while the picture changes from taking a seat to seeing
the nurse come through the door.

The picture leads, the exact Shona prompt sits beneath it, and a quiet
English line helps reviewers. The start screen keeps the recording
disclosure beside the consent button and offers the longer explanation
under “Zvimwe”.

Motion illustrates one action at a time. The adult and child take turns
being underlined, the microphone ring opens gently, and the nurse takes
a small step toward the patient. A check, a ring with a dot, and an empty
ring show completed, current, and upcoming questions. The stills keep
those cues when reduced motion is enabled.
